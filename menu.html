How We Craft Every Melt

From sourdough levain to a molten diagonal cut, here is the secret behind the city's favorite street food crunch.

01

Choose Sourdough

36-hour wild fermented country levain or gluten-friendly artisan bread sliced thick for maximum butter absorption.

02

Molten Cheese Core

Layered with aged Wisconsin cheddar, nutty alpine Gruyère, or gooey mozzarella with custom gourmet fillings.

03

375°F Steam Dome

Seared on seasoned chrome steel under heavy stainless domes for rapid, even melt without drying the interior.

04

Diagonal Dip & Pick Up

Cut cleanly on the bias for instant dunking into piping hot San Marzano tomato bisque. Ready in 5 minutes flat!

Transparency & Care

Dietary & Allergen Guide

We take food allergies and dietary lifestyles with the utmost culinary seriousness. Here is how we ensure your meal is safe and delicious.

🟢

100% Pure Vegetarian Safe

Dedicated vegetarian chrome flat-top grill surface and green-handled spatulas. No meat or poultry juices ever touch our veg bread.

🌾

Gluten-Friendly Substitutes

Certified gluten-tested country artisan loaf available on request for any sandwich (+₹39). Grilled with sanitized dedicated tools.

🥛

Dairy-Free & Vegan Upgrades

Plant-based coconut-oil and cashew mozzarella/cheddar alternatives seared in organic olive oil available for vegan foodies (+₹49).

🥜

100% Tree-Nut & Peanut Free

Our food truck and prep commissary are strictly 100% tree-nut and peanut-free kitchens. Zero peanut or nut oils used.

🍗

Halal-Certified Poultry & Bacon

All chicken and cured meats are 100% farm-traceable, certified halal, free of chemical nitrates, and cured with real hickory wood.

🥣

Naturally Gluten-Free Bisque

Our slow-simmered San Marzano tomato bisque is thickened purely with roasted vegetables and cream. Zero flour roux added!

Got Questions?

Curbside Ordering FAQs

Everything you need to know about our griddle speed, pickup windows, custom orders, and catering.

Every single melt is cooked to order right on our 375°F commercial flat-top griddle! From ticket print to buzzer ring takes approximately 4 to 6 minutes. This allows the heavy steam dome to trap moisture and melt the cheese into gooey perfection without burning the crisp sourdough levain crust.

Absolutely! We welcome cheese customization. You can ask our griddle sear master for extra brown crunch, blend Monterey Jack with 18-month Wisconsin Cheddar, or add fiery pickled jalapeños and sweet bacon jam to any melt on the menu.

Yes! Our famous Fire-Roasted San Marzano Tomato Bisque is 100% vegetarian. It is naturally velvety and thick because it is made from pureed slow-roasted plum tomatoes, fresh sweet basil, and rich dairy cream—with zero flour or wheat roux. Simply request it without sourdough croutons for a 100% gluten-free bowl.

For our Curbside Party Boxes (serving 5 to 12 people), you can add them directly to your cart or call our food truck hotline 30 minutes in advance. We pack them in heavy-duty thermal insulated boxes that maintain crunch and temperature for up to 45 minutes of transit.

We accept all major digital UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), all contactless Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), and cash at our ordering window.

Yes! Our mobile food truck is fully self-powered and self-contained, capable of catering parties from 30 up to 500+ guests. We also set up indoor Interactive Melt Stations and Bisque Shooter Bars. Visit our Catering & Private Events page for package details!
