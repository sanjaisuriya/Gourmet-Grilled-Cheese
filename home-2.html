Behind The Flattop Curbside culinary ritual

The 5:00 AM Bakery Run & Sizzling Steel

Before our truck rolls into your district, our chefs are hand-slicing whole wheels of alpine cheese, simmering twenty gallons of San Marzano tomato bisque, and picking up blistering hot sourdough batards still crackling from the bakery deck.

Every sandwich we sear is balanced like a fine-dining plate: the crunch of slow-caramelized crust, the molten elasticity of grass-fed dairy, and the sweet or acidic punch that cuts through rich warmth.
