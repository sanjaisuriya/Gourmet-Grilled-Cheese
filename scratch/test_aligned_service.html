Got Questions?

Catering Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking our food truck.

Our food truck is approximately 22 feet long, 8.5 feet wide, and 10.5 feet tall. We require relatively level ground on concrete, asphalt, or packed gravel. If you're hosting at a private residence or private venue, please ensure gates and driveways have sufficient clearance.

We are 100% self-sufficient! Our truck carries a commercial, whisper-quiet inverter generator that powers our exhaust hoods, refrigeration, and griddle lights. If your venue has strict sound ordinances, we also carry 100-foot commercial power cords to plug into a standard 220V 32A outlet.

Absolutely! More than 65% of our menu items are naturally pure vegetarian (green dot). We also offer gluten-free artisan bread options on request, as well as plant-based melted cheddar alternatives. Simply note your dietary split in the booking form.

Our heavy commercial chrome griddles can toast 24 sandwiches simultaneously every 4 to 5 minutes. On average, our two-person line cooks and serves 120 to 140 piping hot meals per hour without long queue waiting times.

Catering deposits are refundable up to 14 days before your event. In the case of severe outdoor weather (heavy storms or monsoon warnings), we allow complimentary rescheduling to any available open date within 6 months.
