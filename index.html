✨ OUR MOBILE STORY

BORN ON THE CURBSIDE,
PERFECTED ON THE GRIDDLE

The Melt District started with a simple belief: nostalgic comfort food deserves fine-dining culinary respect. We transformed a vintage 1974 step-van into a mobile high-heat kitchen, equipped with mirror-finish chrome griddles and custom steam dome cloches.

Every morning at 5:00 AM in our central commissary depot, our crew collects fresh 36-hour wild-fermented sourdough from independent artisan bakers, shreds whole wheels of Wisconsin cheddar and French Gruyère by hand, and slow-roasts San Marzano tomatoes for our signature dipping bisque.

Our mission is simple: serve unforgettable gourmet melts made with premium ingredients, handcrafted with passion, and grilled to molten perfection right in front of your eyes.

Artisan Griddle Alchemy

THE SECRET TO THE PERFECT MELT

Four non-negotiable rules our griddle masters follow for every single sandwich that rolls out of our service window.

01 🍞

36-Hr Wild Sourdough

Fermented with wild starter for deep, complex tang. Sliced thick and buttered edge-to-edge for an audible crunch that stays crisp down to the last bite.

Crispy Golden Exterior
02 🧀

Aged Cheese Alchemy

Zero processed plastic singles. We hand-grate sharp Wisconsin cheddar, nutty Gruyère, and fresh whole-milk mozzarella to achieve the legendary 24-inch stretch.

Molten Core • 24" Pull
03 🔥

375°F Chrome Griddle

Sizzled on high-output chrome steel and covered under our steam domes. The trapped heat melts the thick cheese layer without burning the buttery golden bread.

Slow-Roasted Dip
