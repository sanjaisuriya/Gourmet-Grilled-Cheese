Booking Clarity

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about food truck bookings, catering availability, service areas, and reservation timelines.

Our food truck arrives 60 to 90 minutes before your meal service window to position, stabilize jacks, and bring our flat-top griddles to searing temperature. We are completely self-powered with onboard ultra-quiet commercial inverter generators and self-contained fresh water systems, meaning zero exterior electrical or utility hookups are required from your venue.

We comfortably cater events from 50 up to 500+ guests. Equipped with dual 36-inch chrome steel griddles, our experienced line cooks comfortably serve 120 to 160 custom gourmet sourdough melts per hour, keeping guest wait times under 4 minutes. We offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-friendly bread substitutions upon request.

We service the entire Bengaluru metropolitan area and commercial corridors within a 30 km radius of our central commissary kitchen depot in Indiranagar. For private venues, vineyard weddings, or corporate retreats beyond 30 km, custom travel arrangements can be accommodated upon request.

For weekend weddings, corporate milestones, and peak festival dates, we advise reserving your date 3 to 6 weeks in advance to secure your preferred meal slot. For weekday corporate office lunches, we can occasionally accommodate bookings with as little as 1 to 2 weeks notice depending on schedule availability.
